A chapter filled with time, memories and lessons

Two years of us...

18 March 2022 se shuru hua woh safar, jisme humne ek dusre ke saath bahut saare chhote-bade moments share kiye. Kuch din bahut khoobsurat the, kuch mushkil bhi. Lekin har din ne humari kahani mein apni ek jagah banayi.

18 March 2022 · A New Beginning

The day we became an "us".

Us din se humari life mein ek naya chapter shuru hua. Ab sirf baatein nahi thi, balki ek dusre ke liye time nikalna, chhoti-chhoti baatein share karna aur bina kisi wajah ke ek dusre ko yaad karna bhi humari routine ka hissa ban gaya tha.

Shayad us waqt hum dono ko andaza bhi nahi tha ki aane wale waqt mein kitni saari yaadein banne wali hain.

The Days We Shared

Ordinary days became special.

University ke din, hostel ki baatein, saath breakfast karna, ek dusre ki plate se khana, midnight cooking aur woh chhoti-chhoti nok-jhok...

Inmein se shayad kuch bhi duniya ke liye special na ho, lekin mere liye ye moments bahut maayne rakhte hain.

Aapke saath guzara hua normal sa din bhi kabhi-kabhi kisi special occasion se zyada yaad reh jaata tha.

Somewhere Along The Way

We had our own little world.

Ek waqt aisa bhi tha jab humne ek hi jagah rehkar apni daily life share ki. Saath rehna, khana banana, ek dusre ka khayal rakhna aur chhoti-chhoti baaton par hasna...

Aaj peeche mudkar dekhta hoon toh samajh aata hai ki happiness hamesha kisi badi cheez mein nahi hoti. Kabhi-kabhi woh bas kisi apne ke paas hone mein hoti hai.

What I Need To Acknowledge

I know I made mistakes.

Humari kahani mein sab kuch hamesha sahi nahi raha. Mujhse galtiyan hui. Maine aapko woh time, attention aur care nahi di jiski aap deserve karti thi.

Maine kuch aise decisions liye jinka asar aap par pada, aur aapko baar-baar hurt kiya. Aapne mujhe chances bhi diye, lekin main unki value us waqt theek se samajh nahi paaya.

Main apni galtiyon ko kisi reason ke peeche chhupana nahi chahta. Jo hua, uski responsibility meri hai.

Kaash main us waqt samajh paata ki kisi ko pyaar karna sirf kehna nahi, balki har din apne actions se dikhana bhi hota hai.

What These Years Taught Me

Some lessons stay with you.

In do saalon ne mujhe bahut kuch sikhaya. Kisi ke saath hona aur kisi ke liye present rehna, dono alag cheezein hain.

Kisi ki feelings ko samajhna, unki baat sunna, unhe importance dena aur apni galti accept karna bhi utna hi zaroori hai jitna pyaar jatana.

Main ye sab pehle samajh paata, toh shayad kuch cheezein alag hoti.

"Har kahani ka har hissa perfect nahi hota. Lekin kuch kahaniyan humein woh sikha jaati hain jo shayad hum kabhi bhool nahi paate."
