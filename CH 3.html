Chapter 03
The little moments that meant everything
🦋

Our little world...

Dheere-dheere humari life mein kuch aise moments aane lage jo shayad us waqt bahut normal lagte the. Saath breakfast karna, ek-doosre ke saath waqt bitana, chhoti-chhoti shararatein... Aur pta hi nahi chala kab ye sab humari favourite memories ban gayi.

GNA University · Our Everyday Moments

Breakfast was never just breakfast.

Yaad hai jab aap breakfast ke liye merko call kiya karti thi? Aur mai tab tak ready bhi nahi hua hota tha.

Aapka call aata tha aur fir jaldi-jaldi ready hokar breakfast ke liye aana padta tha.

Girls ka boys hostel mess mein aana bhi apne aap mein ek alag hi scene hua karta tha.

Aur sabse cute baat toh tab hoti thi jab aap meri plate se khana lene lagti thi.

"Apki plate se khana zyada tasty lg rha hai."

Pta nahi aapko yaad hai ya nahi, lekin mujhe aaj bhi ye chhoti-chhoti baatein yaad hain.

B Block · 5th Floor

A place that became special.

University mein meri favourite jagah B Block ki fifth floor thi.

Yaad hai ek Saturday ko hum pehli baar wahan ek classroom mein akele mile the?

Weekend par university mein classes nahi hoti thi, lekin us din hum wahan aaye the.

Shayad kisi aur ke liye woh bas ek normal classroom hoga, lekin mere liye us jagah ki apni ek alag importance hai.

Our Little Habits

The comfort of being together.

Mujhe sabse zyada yaad woh moments aate hain jab mai aapki god mein so jaata tha aur jab aankhein khulti thi toh sabse pehle aapka face dikhta tha.

Aapko sona kitna pasand tha, aur mere saamne aap bilkul ek chhote bachche ki tarah behave karti thi.

Mai apne haath se khana bhi nahi khata tha. Aap mujhe apne haath se khilaya karti thi.

Aur chokha... woh bhi maine pehli baar aapke haath se hi khaya tha.

Ye sab sunne mein chhoti baatein lag sakti hain, lekin mere liye inki value kabhi chhoti nahi thi.

Satnampura · Phagwara

A home of our own little memories.

Hostel mein ek semester saath rehne ke baad hum hostel se bahar shift ho gaye the.

Shubham, jise hum Birju bulate the, woh bhi humare saath boys hostel mein rehta tha.

Hostel se shift hone ke lagbhag teen mahine baad hum Satnampura, Phagwara mein saath rehne lage the.

Wahan ki midnight cooking, raat ko saath mein kuch banana aur chhoti-chhoti baaton par hasna... woh sab apne aap mein ek alag hi duniya thi.

Shayad wahi woh waqt tha jab normal daily life bhi kisi khoobsurat memory jaisi lagne lagi thi.

The Little Things I Remember

All the things that made you, you.

Aapko dancing kitni pasand thi. Jab bhi dance ki baat hoti thi, aapka excitement alag hi hota tha.

Aur paneer... aap toh paneer chaar din lagataar bhi kha sakti thi.

Mai aapko Jaana, Jaan aur Dumbo bulata tha. Har naam ke peeche apni ek alag si feeling thi.

Aaj jab in sab baaton ko yaad karta hoon, toh realise hota hai ki kisi insaan ko special banane ke liye hamesha bade moments ki zaroorat nahi hoti.

Kabhi-kabhi uski chhoti-chhoti aadatein hi sabse zyada yaad reh jaati hain.

"Shayad humari duniya bahut badi nahi thi, lekin us chhoti si duniya mein bahut saari khoobsurat yaadein thi." — Our little world
