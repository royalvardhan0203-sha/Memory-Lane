Chapter 02
The moment everything changed
🦋

And then it happened...

Kabhi-kabhi ek conversation zindagi ka sabse khoobsurat mod ban jaati hai. Aur humari kahani mein bhi ek aisi hi conversation hui thi. Shayad aapko yaad ho...

15 or 16 March · 2022

"Yrr mujhse ek galti ho gyi hai..."

Yaad hai aapne merko ek din call kiya tha aur kaha tha ki aapse ek galti ho gayi hai?

Maine pucha tha, kya galti ho gayi? Aur fir aapne jo kaha, uske baad conversation kuch aisi hui thi...

"Mujhe ek ladke se pyar ho gya hai but ptanhi usko merse hai ya ni mai use bol du to wo kya react krega kya ni"

Maine aapse kaha tha ki naam batao, fir sochenge ki usse kaise batana hai.

Lekin mujhe kahan pta tha ki aapka agla jawab meri zindagi ki sabse special baaton mein se ek ban jayega.

Just Three Words

And your answer was...

"Apse."

Bas ek chhota sa jawab tha, lekin mere liye us waqt duniya ki sabse badi baat ban gaya tha.

Shayad us moment mein mere paas kehne ke liye bahut kuch tha, lekin kuch feelings aisi hoti hain jinhe words mein explain karna mushkil hota hai.

Aaj bhi jab us conversation ke baare mein sochta hoon, toh woh moment bilkul waise hi yaad aata hai.

18 March · 2022

The day I said yes.

Aur fir aaya 18 March 2022 ka din, jab maine aapke proposal ko accept kiya tha.

Us din se humari kahani ek naye chapter mein enter kar gayi thi.

Aur proposal ke baad jab hum pehli baar mile, toh sixth floor ki stairs par aapne mujhe pehli baar hug kiya tha.

Pta nahi aapko woh moment kitna yaad hai, lekin mujhe lagta hai ki hum dono us hug ko kabhi nahi bhool payenge.

Woh sirf ek hug nahi tha. Mere liye woh humari kahani ka ek aisa moment tha jo hamesha mere saath rahega.

"Kuch moments waqt ke saath purane nahi hote, bas yaadon mein aur gehre hote jaate hain." — 18 March 2022
