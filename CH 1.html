How it all began...

Humein tab kahan pta tha ki university ka ek normal sa din humari kahani ki shuruaat ban jayega. Na koi plan tha, na kuch socha tha. Bas sab kuch apne aap ho gaya.

GNA University · 2021

A simple hello. An unexpected beginning.

Yaad hai jab hum pehli baar mile the? University ki classes, corridors aur roz ke normal se dinon ke beech humari mulaqat hui thi.

Aap merse ek number maangne aayi thi, aur bas wahi ek chhoti si baat humari conversations ki wajah ban gayi. Tab hum dono ko kahan pta tha ki ye baatein humein kahan tak le jayengi.

Dheere-dheere humari baatein badhne lagi. University ke baad jab aap apne hostel chali jaati thi, toh mai aapko call karta tha. Fir pta hi nahi chalta tha ki kitni der tak baat karte rehte the hum.

Pta nahi kya baat thi un conversations mein, lekin aapse baat karke ek normal sa din bhi thoda khaas lagne lagta tha.

A little pause

Even silence couldn't end it.

Aur fir jab sab kuch thoda familiar hone laga tha, ek din maine achanak aapse baat karna band kar diya aur Chandigarh chala gaya.

Zindagi apni tarah chalti rahi, din beet te gaye. Fir March 2022 mein ek din aapka call aaya. Aapki tabiyat kharab thi aur aapne merko call kiya tha.

Shayad us waqt hum dono ko bhi andaza nahi tha ki humari kahani mein abhi kitna kuch baaki tha.

"Kabhi-kabhi shuruaat itni normal hoti hai, ki us waqt ehsaas hi nahi hota ki woh kitni khaas banne wali hai." — A little piece of us
